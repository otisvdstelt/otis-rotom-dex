ROTOM'S VOICE CLIPS
===================

One folder per kind of line. Save each recording as an .mp3 under exactly the
file name shown. Leave the opening "BZZZT!" out of the recording: the app
plays that as a buzz.

[x] = recorded and in the app      [ ] = still to make


greeting/   said once, when Rotom has finished starting up
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
[x] greeting.mp3   Rotom Dex, at your service! Tell me a Pokémon's name or
                   number and I'll look it up. Or open my menu for berries,
                   snacks, moves, badges and more-roto!
