# Why I built Rotom Dex

This is my third Pokédex. First a sprite one, then a 3D device. This time I wanted the device to be a character, so I picked Rotom. Looking something up should feel like asking Rotom: it scans, reacts with its whole body, and shows the answer on its own screen.

## My rules

- It has to look like the real Rotom Dex from the anime, with a small compact grin.
- Every visit starts with Rotom asleep, and you wake it yourself.
- On a phone, the phone is Rotom.
- All data goes on Rotom's screen, never in panels next to it.
- It should feel like the Pokémon world, never like a generic AI website.
- Cel shaded 3D, clean outlines, electric glow, a glass key tray with blue and yellow.
- Moods are fixed: idle amber, scanning cyan, success green, error red.
- No fake data. Unbuilt databanks show as empty slots.

## Good to know

Data and images come from PokéAPI. Gym badge info is kept by hand. The voice is recorded clips made with a voice model, with no browser speech. There's no backend and no accounts, and everything works with a keyboard and screen readers.
